## Learning Radio Astronomy

The European Radio Interferometry School 2022 was a one-week workshop on radio astronomy data acquisition, reduction, and analysis. The event was sponsored by [OPTICON-RadioNET Pilot](https://www.orp-h2020.eu/) and hosted by the Joint Institute for VLBI ERIC ([JIVE](https://jive.eu/)) and the Netherlands Institute for Radio Astronomy ([ASTRON](https://www.astron.nl/)).

The lectures and tutorials covered calibration and imaging, interferometry across multiple wavelength ranges, astronomical data analysis, and observing-plan design.

## Tutorials

The [basic tutorials](https://www.jb.man.ac.uk/DARA/ERIS22/) provide hands-on practice with radio-interferometric data, including CASA installation and tutorial datasets.
