This page summarizes my observing programs as a principal investigator, lead observer, and co-investigator, as well as other observing experience.

## Approved Programs as Principal Investigator/Lead

- **2022 – VLT/FORS2:** The hunt for gravitationally lensed quasars in the early Universe. 4.7 hours; program 110.243U.002.
- **2019 – VLT/MUSE:** Probing the environment of the highest-accreting supermassive black hole at *z* > 6. 5 hours; program 0104.B-0665(A).
- **2019 – ALMA:** What is the environment of the highest-accreting black hole at *z* > 6? 2 hours; program 2019.1.01436.S.

## Selected Programs as Co-Investigator

- **2023 – Gemini/GMOS-S:** Confirming the highest-redshift AGN acting as a strong gravitational lens. 2 hours; GS-2023A-FT-111 (PI: J. Chan).
- **2021 – JWST/NIRSpec and NIRCam:** A complete census of supermassive black holes and hosts. 49.5 hours; program 01967 (PI: M. Onoue).
- **2021 – GTC/EMIR and NOT/NOTCam:** The Canarias search for the first quasars. 72 hours with GTC and 10 nights with NOT (PI: E. Bañados).
- **2021 – NOEMA:** Molecular-line scan of the infrared-luminous quasar PJ083+11 at *z* = 6.34. 20 hours; S21DI (PI: R. Decarli).
- **2019 – HST/WFC3:** Testing the accretion and lensing interpretations of PSOJ083+11. 4 orbits; GO 15707 (PI: K. Jahnke).

## Observing Experience

- **2020–2021 – LBT/MODS and MPG/ESO 2.2m/GROND:** Search for reionization-era quasars; more than 100 hours in visitor mode (PI: E. Bañados).
- **2020 – VLT/FORS2:** Confirmation of reionization-era quasars; 2 nights, program 106.20WQ (PI: E. Bañados).
- **2019 – Gemini-N/GNIRS:** Confirmation of an active black hole at *z* = 6.34; 3 hours, GN-2019A-FT-204 (PI: M. Onoue).
- **2019 – NOT/NOTCam:** Search for *z* > 6.5 quasars; 5 nights, program 59-015 (PI: R. Decarli).
- **2019 – Rozhen 2m telescope:** Optical intranight variability of gamma-ray blazars; 30 hours during the NEON Observing School.
- **2019 – Calar Alto 1.23m telescope:** Nearby exoplanet search; 10 nights of remote observing through Project EDEN.
- **2017 – Thai National Observatory 2.4m/MRES:** Spectroscopy of binary AGN candidates; 3 nights.
- **2013–2018 – Bosscha Observatory, 20–70cm telescopes:** Observations of transiting exoplanets, novae, nebulae, and other emission-line objects; more than 20 nights per year.
